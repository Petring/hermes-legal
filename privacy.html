Privacy Policy — Hermes

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Hermes is a personal AI assistant tool built and operated by its sole owner for their own private use. It is not distributed to or used by the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's own Google account authorization, Hermes may read and send email, and read and manage calendar events, strictly to carry out tasks the owner asks it to perform (e.g. drafting replies, scheduling events, retrieving information).

How data is used

Data accessed through this authorization is used only to perform the owner's requested tasks. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train general-purpose machine learning models.

Storage

Any data cached locally (e.g. message content needed mid-task) is stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner and retained only as long as needed to complete the relevant task.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the account owner directly.
